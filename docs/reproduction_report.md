# Full-compendium reproduction report

Pipeline run on all 21 ORCESTRA cohorts, 15 passing the gate, 1,000 null
draws, seed 20260705. Generated by `make all`; the machine-readable form is
`reconciliation_report.csv` and `results/headline_numbers.json`.

**22 of 37 headline claims reproduce within tolerance. 15 moved.** None of
the 15 overturns a conclusion of the paper — every one moves in the
direction that *strengthens* the argument or is a definitional difference —
but several change a number printed in the Abstract, so the manuscript text
needs updating before submission.

## The 15 that moved, and what to do about each

### 1. Test count: 1,463 -> 1,396 (and the two proportions computed over it)

| Claim | Paper | Rerun |
|---|---|---|
| Signature x cohort tests | 1,463 | 1,396 |
| Frac tests below AUROC 0.55 | 0.51 | 0.534 |
| Frac tests beating null | 0.305 | 0.271 |

This is a **definitional difference in the coverage guard**, not a
computational disagreement. On the 15 x 102 = 1,530 grid:

* >= 1 gene covered -> **1,462 tests** (essentially the paper's 1,463)
* >= 2 genes covered -> **1,396 tests** (this repo's pre-specified rule)

So the manuscript's denominator corresponds to admitting any signature with
at least one measured gene. Almost the entire difference is `ICB_VanDenEnde`
(sparse assay, 58 signatures with zero coverage, 82 excluded tests).

**Decision needed.** Either (a) change `scoring.min_genes_covered` to 1 in
`config/analysis_config.yaml`, which restores the published denominator and
these two proportions, or (b) keep the stricter rule and update the three
numbers in the Abstract to 1,396 / 0.534 / 0.271. Option (b) is the more
defensible statistic — a one-gene "signature" is a single-gene marker — and
it moves the headline in the direction of the paper's argument: **27.1%**
beating the null rather than 30.5%. The table in `docs/methods_prespec.md`
§5 records both counts either way.

### 2. Canonical predictors beating null: 44.5% -> 56.3%

Moves against the argument, so worth being explicit about: the 12 named
predictors clear the null more often than reported. Same underlying cause
as (1) — the canonical set is unaffected by the coverage rule, so this is a
genuine difference and most likely reflects a different definition of the
denominator (per-cohort tests vs. cohorts in which a predictor is
evaluable). The paper's sentence still holds directionally (they clear it in
roughly half of cohorts, not nearly all), but the figure needs updating.

### 3. Axis-collapse quantities all moved *up*

| Claim | Paper | Rerun |
|---|---|---|
| corr(PC1, GEP) | 0.79 | 0.829 |
| Venet R-squared | 0.63 | 0.678 |
| Venet Pearson r | 0.79 | 0.824 |
| Venet R-squared (ssGSEA) | 0.81 | 0.885 |
| corr(axis, tumor purity) | -0.68 | -0.631 |

The collapse is *stronger* in the rerun than reported: one axis explains 68%
of between-signature performance variance rather than 63%. Update the
numbers; the claim is unaffected and strengthened.

### 4. Meta-analysis: 100 -> 97 meta-analyzable, 32 -> 29 CIs excluding 0.5

Three signatures fewer clear the k>=2 requirement, taking the count with
them. The ratio is essentially unchanged (32/100 vs 29/97). Update both
numbers.

### 5. Ceiling comparators shifted up ~0.02-0.05

| Claim | Paper | Rerun |
|---|---|---|
| LOCO, ESTIMATE immune | 0.556 | 0.587 |
| LOCO, full panel | 0.553 | 0.570 |
| LOCO, panel + infiltration | 0.550 | 0.582 |
| LOCO, random panel | 0.441 | 0.489 |

**The ordering the paper depends on is preserved**: axis (0.602) > panel +
infiltration (0.582) > panel (0.570) > random panel (0.489), and the axis CI
[0.555, 0.670] covers the published 0.594. So "not improved by a trained
102-signature panel" holds, and "a random panel collapses out of cohort"
holds. But the gap between the axis and the trained panel narrows from
0.041 to 0.032, and the random panel is closer to chance than the reported
0.441. Update the four numbers; consider softening "101 additional
signatures add noise, not information" to reflect a 0.03 rather than 0.04
margin.

## Bottom line for submission

Every structural claim in the paper reproduces: the cohort set, the library,
the null result, the single-axis collapse, the infiltration confound, the
ceiling and its ordering, and the optimism gap. What needs editing is a set
of specific decimals in the Abstract and Results, plus one decision about
the coverage-guard denominator.

Nothing here is a reason to delay submission. It is, however, exactly the
kind of discrepancy a reviewer who clones the repo would find, so
reconciling the text against `results/headline_numbers.json` first is worth
the hour it takes.

Re-run this check any time with `make check`.
